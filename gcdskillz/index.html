Overview

Dates: Thursday 28 January and Thursday 4 February 2027  ·  Workshops: 14  ·  Sessions: 27  ·  Length: 3 hours, morning or afternoon

GCD Skillz is an annual programme of intensive workshops led by tutors and technical staff from across CSM. It sits between two course units, so it isn't linked to any brief or assessment. Instead, it's a space for exploration and experimentation: try something new, gain practical experience, and take what you learn back into your own practice.

In short
  • Sign up for at least one workshop on each Thursday: one on 28 January and one on 4 February, morning or afternoon.
  • First come, first served. Each workshop has a limited number of places.
  • Booking is a commitment. If you can't attend, remove your name in advance so someone else can take your place.

Booking is a commitment

Once you've signed up, please commit to attending. If you can't make it for any reason, remove your name from the Moodle sign-up in advance so the place can go to someone else. Please be considerate to staff and other students.

On the day

  1. Find your room in advance. Most workshops take place outside the GCD studios, so check where you need to be before the day.
  2. Record your attendance in F201 via the SEATS app, at least 10 minutes before your workshop starts. Staff will be there if you have any questions.
  3. Be on time. If you arrive late, you can't join the session and you'll be recorded as absent.
  4. Enjoy!

A Printmaker's Tale: Experimental Woodcuts

Sessions

  • Thursday 4 February 2027 · 10:00–13:00 · E309 · 12 places

Based around a series of woodcut prints made for the book A Printmaker's Tale, this workshop introduces a more open-ended, fluid approach to multi-colour, multi-layer woodcut printing and image making. Using the original blocks cut for the book, you'll reuse and reappropriate them, printing them in different configurations to create new imagery. As well as woodblocks, you'll also work with relief collagraphs, photopolymer plates and masking techniques.

Museum & Study Collection: Rose visual analysis

Sessions

  • Thursday 28 January 2027 · 10:00–13:00 · Meet at CSM Reception, near the Gallery entrance · 12 places
  • Thursday 4 February 2027 · 10:00–13:00 · Meet at CSM Reception, near the Gallery entrance · 12 places
  • Thursday 4 February 2027 · 14:00–17:00 · Meet at CSM Reception, near the Gallery entrance · 12 places

This session introduces the object-based learning methodology of Rose visual analysis. Drawing on the work of Gillian Rose, this methodology is used to analyse fine art and graphic images such as prints, photographs, paintings and collages. You'll try the technique on works from the CSM Museum & Study Collection and learn more about the collection and how you can use it in your work.

You'll then explore the Rose technique further through a making task, using a collection of repurposed, duplicate 19th-century reproduction prints from the museum. You'll manipulate, remove from and add to the prints to reflect the new skills you've learnt. More about Rose visual analysis: Read more →

FAQ

Do I have to take part?

Yes. GCD Skillz is part of your course: sign up for at least one workshop on each Thursday. Which workshops you choose is up to you.

Is it assessed?

No. It isn't linked to any brief or assessment. It's a space to try new things, and what you learn can feed into your own practice.

Can I do more than one workshop on a day?

Yes, a morning and an afternoon session, once Round 2 opens and places are available to everyone.

The workshop I want is full. What now?

Check whether it runs on the other Thursday or in the other slot, or pick something new. Places sometimes free up, so keep an eye on Moodle.

I signed up but can't make it.

Remove your name from the Moodle sign-up as early as you can, so the place can go to someone else.

What should I bring?

If a workshop needs anything from you, it is listed under "Before you come" on that workshop. Some ask you to bring a laptop, photographs or drawing materials.
